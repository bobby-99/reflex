Reflex is a calm, local-first productivity assistant for Android designed around tactile design, frosted glass aesthetics, and absolute privacy.

Features:
• Step-by-step sequential routines with audio cues, confetti, and starter templates.
• Smart tasks with priority levels (P1–P4), recurrence rules, and exact alarm notifications.
• Daily habit tracking with numeric goals, annual contribution heatmaps, and streak statistics.
• Deep focus timers with Pomodoro, interval, and open flow sessions, accompanied by ambient audio ticks.
• Distraction shield with app blocking overlay to stay in flow.
• Unified daily calendar linking routines, scheduled tasks, and device calendars.
• 6 curated color themes based on Design System v1.0.
• Complete data sovereignty: 100% offline Room database, no network permissions, and full JSON export/import.

Reflex is free and open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
